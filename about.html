+ Our story
Adalytical®

Built on the inside. Run for your bottom line.

Most agencies learn Google Ads from the outside. We learned it from the inside — spending years within Google’s own Ads team, seeing exactly how the platform, the algorithms and the internal systems really work.

What we saw over and over was the same thing: generalist agencies leaving millions of dollars on the table for their clients — mismanaged budgets, broken tracking, set-and-forget campaigns. We started Adalytical to fix that, for good.

Since then we’ve audited and optimised 1,000+ accounts, trained 30+ other top agencies, and become one of the fastest-growing Google Ads agencies in the world — not by doing more channels, but by doing one channel better than anyone else.

What we believe

Profit beats vanity. A report full of clicks and impressions means nothing if it isn’t making you money. We measure ourselves on ROAS, cost per acquisition and real revenue — the numbers that actually matter.

And we back it with alignment. No lock-in contracts. Month-to-month, always. You stay because the numbers keep climbing — never because you’re stuck.
